At fifteen, the length approaches essay-like proportions. This text doesn’t merely describe or narrate but also reflects. Think about the purpose of length in writing: sometimes it’s to clarify, sometimes to persuade, sometimes to immerse. A single idea can be unfolded gradually, explained with analogies, and tied back to broader themes. For example, the traveler’s journey could symbolize human learning itself—small steps leading to great distances. That symbolism adds both depth and size to the text.
